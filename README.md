# Kernix Raw — one-knob multi-FX mangler

![Kernix Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/KernixRaw/main/kernixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up and your sound gets driven, echoed, and crushed — never louder.**

Kernix Raw is the free one-knob version of **Kernix Pro**. A single **MANGLE** macro drives a hidden multi-effect engine so you can wreck vocals, synths, drums, or a full mix in one move.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/KernixRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`KernixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **MANGLE** knob is the whole plugin. Turn it up and it front-loads saturation, opens up a re-saturated feedback echo, and bites in bitcrush grit with a touch of detune — all under the hood. Auto-gain runs internally to keep the result level-matched, so it gets dirtier and bigger without jumping in volume. It's the only automatable control, so mapping it in your DAW stays dead simple.

# Kernix Raw: one-knob multi-FX mangler

![Kernix Raw free one-knob multi-FX mangler UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/KernixRaw/main/kernixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up and your sound gets driven, echoed, and crushed, never louder.**

Kernix Raw is the free one-knob version of **Kernix Pro**. A single **MANGLE** macro drives a hidden multi-effect engine so you can wreck vocals, synths, drums, or a full mix in one move.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/KernixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`KernixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Kernix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`KernixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Kernix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **MANGLE** knob is the whole plugin. Turn it up and it front-loads saturation, opens up a re-saturated feedback echo, and bites in bitcrush grit with a touch of detune, all under the hood. Auto-gain runs internally to keep the result level-matched, so it gets dirtier and bigger without jumping in volume. It's the only automatable control, so mapping it in your DAW stays dead simple.
